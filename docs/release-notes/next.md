- Open a paired device from Home and it now says, under its name, whether this device reaches it right now and why not, with a Pairing and removal button that opens its card.
- **Updates no longer get stuck.** A build that was downloaded and waiting for you used to stop the app from looking for anything newer. Now a newer release replaces it, and you install only the newest one. **Check for updates** now always says what it found.
- **Remove always answers.** It finishes within 45 seconds with "Removed" or the reason it didn't, instead of sitting at "Removing…".
- **Pairing a phone finishes even if you're slow to tap Accept.** The phone now waits the full 5 minutes and says "Tap Accept on the computer"; the computer shows the request within a few seconds of the scan.
- **No screen lock? No problem.** Phones and tablets without a fingerprint or screen lock no longer refuse to add a device; your tap in the app is the confirmation.
- **Diagnostic log.** Computers and phones now record pairing, connections (and why they fail), removals and update checks, so problems can be traced instead of guessed.
